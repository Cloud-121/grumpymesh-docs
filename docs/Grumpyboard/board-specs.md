## Built by the community

This board was built by the Gulf Coast Mesh community, on what has been learned on from over 300 repeater deployments.

### Power management

The board accepts a 5v input for power, either via USB or the 5v power connector. When you purchuse a Grumpy Mesh Board, you'll recieve a waveshare solar controller, and all the cables needed to connect it to the board.

Once connected, The board will be able to not only be powered via a battery with solar. But also be able to monitor the voltage of the battery, unlike every other repeater out there that uses a external solar board.

The advantages of this, is a robust, and reliable repeater. The board can fully drain out it's battiers in the rare event of no sun for days, and still auto recover when sun comes back.

### Wi-Fi and OTA updates

The board also includes an external antenna for your board, but it also allows you to add any wifi antenna with a ipex connector; For long range over the air updates.

### Expansion port

The board includes a Expansion port, This allowing you to add additional accessories like temperature sensors to monitor your enclosure or outside temperature, a wind sensor, rain sensor, and almost anything you'd like. With open documentation on how to use it, the possibilities are endless.
