## Thank you for buying a Grumpy Board

### This guide will explain to you how to setup, and wire your Grumpyboard from start to finish.


Each board purchased comes with the following:

* 1x GrumpyBoard
* 1x Waveshare Solar Board
* 2x JST-PH Power Leads
* 1x Wi-Fi antenna
* 1x Quality Control Card
* 1x Getting Started Guide (Brought you here)

---------------------

To get started with your board, you'll need the following:

* A computer running Linux, Windows, or Mac OS.
* A USB C cable that can handle data and power.
* A SMA or IPEX antenna that's in the 915 MHz range.


## How to flash your board.

- **Unbox your board:** Remove the GrumpyBoard from its packaging.
- **Connect the antenna:** Insert the included IPEX WiFi antenna into the ESP32 connector, positioned near the USB-C port.
- **Attach your antenna:** Connect your SMA or IPEX antenna to the board using either the SMA mount on top or the IPEX port beneath it.
- **Connect via USB-C:** Plug a USB-C cable into the GrumpyBoard and connect the other end to your computer.
- **Put the board into boot mode:** Hold down the left button on the grumpy board, then press the right button once; after let go of the left button and your board will now be in boot mode.
- **Visit the flashing portal:** Navigate to `Grumpymesh.com/flasher` in your web browser.
- **Choose firmware:** Select the firmware type you prefer: **Client**, **Repeater**.
- **Select if you'd like wifi configuration:** If you choose Repeater, you will be asked if you'd like to configure the board via a wifi web portal or not. If you choose to enable it, once the board reboots for the first time; it will make a WiFi access point called "Grumpymesh". You can then use this portal to configure the board's settings via a phone or computer.
- **Flash the firmware**: Click erase and then click the flash button. Your browser will ask at the top left to select the device.
- **Wait 1-2 minutes**: After the flashing process is complete, you will now have a flashed GrumpyBoard ready to use.
